Description of Contents

This repository contains all code, data, and figures for the Conceptual Engineering Word Embeddings Study.

Folders & Files

Word Embeddings/ – Preprocessed embeddings for target words.
experiments/ – Experimental results (PCA, clustering, metrics).
context_groups/ – Clustered contexts for each word.
summary_table.csv – Metrics for all words, including taxonomy for lexical categorisation.
wordlist.yaml – List of all analysed words.
imgs/ – Figures: consensus clustering landscapes, metrics comparisons, silhouette & PCA plots.
notebooks/ –
Figures_Chapter_6.ipynb – Figures for thesis Chapter 6.
bnc_conceptual_engineering.ipynb – Main experimental workflow.

Target Words
cable, camera, cancer, child, computer, concept, data, duty, economy, education, electricity, energy, engineer, exam, family, freedom, friend, hat, information, knowledge, marriage, plane, planet, pressure, protein, responsibility, student, system, temperature, theory, tiger, truth, water, weight, wheel, wife

All data are anonymised and fully reproducible.
