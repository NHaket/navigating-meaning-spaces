DESCRIPTION OF CONTENTS
Conceptual Engineering Questionnaire Study

This repository contains the materials and datasets for the Conceptual Engineering Questionnaire Study, including participant documents, the questionnaire instrument, and anonymised response data for each target word.

DOCUMENTS

Participant Information Sheet.pdf
Outlines the study’s purpose, participant rights, and data-handling procedures.

Questionnaire.pdf
The full questionnaire instrument, including all instructions and the five elicitation tasks.

DATA FILES (.xlsx)
Each Excel file corresponds to one target word (e.g., system, theory, freedom) and contains six tabs:
    All Responses – Complete dataset of all participant answers
    ExQ (Examples Question) – Examples of things participants consider instances of the word
    DefQ (Definition Question) – Participant definitions or explanations
    AssocQ (Associations Question) – Related words and ideas
    CharQ (Characteristics Question) – Features or qualities that define the word
    CntxQ (Contexts Question) – Situations or settings where the word is used

Each question tab includes categorised responses with corresponding Participant Reach (PR) and Elaboration Count (EC) values used in the thesis analysis.
